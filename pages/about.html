About Not Yo Mama's Magazine

Bridging Heritage and New Horizons. A Black-owned, diaspora-first magazine built to feel like a premiere, not a page—where culture, luxury, and community sit at the same table.

Mission

We champion the brilliance of the Black diaspora—celebrating heritage, spotlighting innovators, and telling stories with the swagger of Jet, Essence, and Ebony, remixed for right now.

What We Cover

Community Pulse Spotlight Covers Money & Power Beauty & Style Music & Film Wellness & Joy Heritage & History Photo Essays Directory

Every story carries a diaspora lens—rooted in legacy, elevated in presentation, and crafted to feel immersive.

Voice & Values

  • Luxurious, not distant. Cinematic visuals, tactile textures, and considered design that still feels lived-in.
  • Heritage-forward. We honor lineage—archival nods, oral histories, and elders beside tomorrow’s icons.
  • Community-led. Reader letters, submissions, and Black-owned directories keep us accountable to the culture.
  • Global Black lens. From Lagos to Atlanta to London to Kingston—stories cross borders, rhythms, and languages.
